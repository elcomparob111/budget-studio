Shared budgets

Only invite someone you trust. A member can view, change, and export the shared budget. Changes can sync to the other member’s devices, and the most recent save may replace an earlier edit.

If a member leaves, the app creates a personal copy that keeps that member’s own and unassigned entries while removing partner-attributed transactions. If the owner deletes the shared budget, other members lose access. Members may still have copies or exports made before they left or the budget was deleted.
